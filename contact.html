Prospective Students & Postdocs

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students and postdoctoral researchers interested in coral genomics, disease ecology, microbiome science, or marine conservation.

To inquire about opportunities, please send a brief email with your CV and a short description of your research interests to svollmer@fau.edu.

For media inquiries, contact FAU Media Relations at mediarelations@fau.edu.
