The Vollmer Lab

Research

Genomics, microbiomes, and conservation — from molecules to reefs.

Research area 01

Coral Resilience Genetics

We identify the genes and genomic regions that help corals survive White Band Disease and adapt to warming oceans. Using genome-wide association studies, we've linked 73 SNPs and 10 genomic regions to disease resistance in Acropora cervicornis — giving restoration programs a genomic tool to select for resilience.

Research area 02

Coral Microbiome Characterization

We use metagenomics and machine learning to distinguish beneficial coral symbionts from harmful pathogens. Our work has identified key Vibrio species and other opportunistic pathogens driving White Band Disease, and shown that prophylactic antibiotic treatment can reduce infection rates by 30 percentage points by suppressing the bacterial pathobiome.

Research area 03

Conservation Solutions

We partner with restoration organizations to translate genomic discoveries into field action. Polygenic resistance scores derived from our GWAS work can predict disease resistance in individual coral fragments — enabling smarter, evidence-based selection of restoration genotypes for reef outplanting.

Methods & Approaches

Genomic Technologies

Whole-genome sequencing, RAD-seq, RNA-seq, small RNA profiling, GWAS, genome assembly & annotation.

Computational Biology

Population genomics, machine learning, network analysis, bioinformatics pipelines, statistical modeling.

Experimental Systems

Field collections, disease challenge experiments, antibiotic treatments, coral tank systems, microbiome manipulation.

Model Organisms

Acropora cervicornis (staghorn coral), Acropora palmata, Pocillopora damicornis, and their hybrids.
